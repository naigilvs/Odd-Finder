How the ranking works

Est. win %: every other sportsbook's price for the same line, with its built-in margin (the vig) removed, averaged together. Low-margin books such as Pinnacle count more. This consensus is usually a better estimate of the real chance than any single book.

Book's implied %: the chance the chosen sportsbook's odds imply (1 ÷ decimal odds), margin included.

Expected value: est. win % × decimal odds − 1. +3% means that, if the estimate is right, the bet returns $3 per $100 on average over many bets. Most bets show a negative number because of the margin; positive ones are where the book is out of line with the market.

A high win probability alone is not a good bet. A 90% favorite at odds that imply 95% loses money over time. That's why the list is ranked by expected value, and you can filter by win probability.

Estimates only: no bet is guaranteed and odds change constantly. Check the line in the app before you bet. Only bet what you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
