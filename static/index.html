How Odd Finder ranks bets

Market win chance

For each bet, Odd Finder looks up the same line at up to 9 other sportsbooks, strips out each book's built-in margin, and averages them. Sharp books like Pinnacle count extra because their prices are the most accurate.

Chance of success

The bot's single best guess at how often the bet hits: 75% the market's estimate and 25% the stats', or the market's alone when there are no stats. The market gets more weight because it tested as more accurate.

Confidence

How much to trust that number. It's higher when many sportsbooks offer the same line, when sharp books like Pinnacle are among them, and when market and stats agree. It's lower for player props and when the two opinions disagree. Hover over the badge to see why.

Stats win chance

A second opinion from this season's and last season's results: team strength ratings, points scored and allowed, and home advantage. It doesn't know about injuries or news, so treat it as a check on the market, not a replacement. Covers moneylines, spreads and totals in the NFL, NBA, NHL and MLB.

Book's price

The chance your sportsbook's odds imply, margin included. On each bet, the red tick on the bar marks it. When the bar runs past the tick, the bet's real chance is higher than the price suggests.

Value

Market win chance × decimal odds − 1. A value of +5% means the bet returns about $5 per $100 on average over many bets like it. Most bets land below zero because of the margin. Market + stats agree marks bets where both opinions say the chance is higher than the price implies.
